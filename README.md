I work mostly on empirical asset pricing and portfolio construction: factor models, separating skill from luck in returns, dimensionality reduction on asset universes, and credit risk. Every project below starts from a question, uses public data, and keeps a strict in-sample / out-of-sample split.

---

### Featured projects

| Project | Question | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| [**ARK Invest: Skill or Luck?**](https://github.com/fenicola/Ark-Invest-skill-or-luck) | Did Cathie Wood's ETFs generate alpha, or just ride factor exposure? | Carhart 4-factor OLS, rolling regressions, bootstrap luck distribution |
| [**Meme-Stock Bubbles**](https://github.com/fenicola/meme-stocks-speculative-bubbles) | Were GME, AMC, BB and NOK statistically in a bubble, and when? | PSY (2015) BSADF/GSADF test, Monte Carlo critical values, date stamping |
| [**Crypto Eigen-Portfolios**](https://github.com/fenicola/crypto-eigenportfolio-pca) | What economic forces drive the top 50 cryptocurrencies? | PCA, Marchenko–Pastur denoising, K-Means, attribution to BTC/VIX/gold/geopolitical risk |
| [**S&P 500 Long-Short**](https://github.com/fenicola/sp500-pca-kmeans-longshort) | Can latent factor clusters drive a long-short strategy? | PCA + K-Means on factor loadings, out-of-sample backtest 2023–2025 |
| [**Credit Risk with Neural Networks**](https://github.com/fenicola/Neural-Network) | Which model maximises a bank's expected income on imbalanced default data? | Neural networks in R, stepwise selection, cut-off optimisation on business metrics |
| [**Return Analysis: NVIDIA**](https://github.com/fenicola/Return-Analysis) | How do NVDA's risk and market beta evolve over time? | CAPM, rolling beta, VaR, interactive [Streamlit dashboard](https://return-analysis-nvidia-1.streamlit.app) |

**Some findings**
- None of the five ARK ETFs shows alpha beyond what luck explains once factor exposures are controlled for; about 80% of their monthly returns are explained by four factors.
- GameStop's 2021 episode is a clear bubble (GSADF 3.79 vs a 1.32 critical value); Nokia shows no explosive dynamics at all.
